Series ID entered: **DGS10**. Comparison dates: **30 September 2026** (last day of last month) and **29 October 2026** (today).

## Source and meaning

- **Source:** Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, H.15 Selected Interest Rates, distributed by FRED and ALFRED at the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
- **Series page:** [DGS10 on FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10).
- **Current download:** [FRED public CSV](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10).
- **Historical download:** [ALFRED vintage for 30 September 2026](https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/graph/alfredgraph.csv?id=DGS10&vintage_date=2026-09-30).
- **What it measures:** Market yield on U.S. Treasury securities at a constant maturity of 10 years, quoted on an investment basis. This is a yield, not a bond price or a realized investment return.
- **Units and frequency:** Percent per year; daily; not seasonally adjusted. For example, 5.05 means 5.05%, not 505%. Missing observations (`.`), including holidays, are excluded from the counts.
- **Download date:** 29 October 2026; current CSV retrieved at **21:09:13 UTC** (17:09:13 EDT).

**This daily market rate shows almost no lag—about one business day in these FRED/ALFRED snapshots—and no revisions in the comparison.** On September 30, September 29 was the latest available observation. Today, October 28 is the latest available observation. The FRED page reports an October 29, 2026, 3:16 PM CDT update for that October 28 value.

The 20 observations that disappear when switching from today's vintage to September 30 had not yet been published in that vintage. They include later market dates and the September 30 observation itself, which was not yet available in that snapshot. Their disappearance is publication timing, not a revision. The revised count of zero means all nonmissing dates shared by the September 30 vintage and today's download have identical values. It does not prove the series can never be corrected; FRED explicitly notes that data are subject to revision.

The app's main charts always show today's values; changing the as-known date changes the vintage endpoint and comparison table. “Disappear” here refers to the historical vintage's available observations. A date-level vintage does not establish availability before a particular intraday trade.

The [Federal Reserve H.15 release page](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h15/) states that releases are posted Monday through Friday at 4:15 p.m., excluding holidays and Board closures. Its October 29 release lists market observations through October 28, consistent with the one-business-day lag observed here.
